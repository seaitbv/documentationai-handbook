## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unbox and Inspect">
    Remove all packaging. Ensure parts are included and undamaged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power On">
    Connect to a power source. Press the power button until the LED lights up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Sea-it App">
    Download from the App Store or Google Play. Install and open the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair Genius">
    Navigate to Devices > Add New Device. Hold the pairing button on the trailer for 3 seconds, then connect in app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">You’re ready to roll! For detailed setup, see the user manual.</Callout>
