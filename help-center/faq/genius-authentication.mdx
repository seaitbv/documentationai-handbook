## Overview

Pairing connects the Sea-it genius trailer with your smartphone via Bluetooth.

## Steps

1. Power on the trailer control unit.
2. Open the Sea-it app on your phone.
3. Go to Devices > Add New Device.
4. Press the pairing button on the trailer for 3 sec.
5. The trailer LED should flash blue.
6. The app will detect "Genius Trailer". Tap to connect.
7. Wait for confirmation. LED turns solid blue.

<Callout kind="info">If prompted for a PIN, enter `1234`.</Callout>
