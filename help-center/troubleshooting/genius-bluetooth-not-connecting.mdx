---
title: Bluetooth Not Connecting
description: Fix Bluetooth pairing issues with Genius.
---

## Symptoms

- Genius not found in Sea-it app
- Unable to connect via Bluetooth

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Restart Bluetooth">
    Turn Bluetooth off/on on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset Trailer">
    Hold the Genius pairing button for 10 sec to reset connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry Pairing">
    In the Sea-it app: Devices > Add New Device > Follow instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">Move closer to the Genius trailer for best results.</Callout>
