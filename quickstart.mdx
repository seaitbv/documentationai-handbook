## Get Started in 5 Minutes

Get up and running with Sea-it quickly. This guide walks you through finding local dealers, browsing available stock vehicles, submitting inquiries, and preparing for delivery. No prior experience required.

<Callout kind="info">

**Prerequisites**

- Access to the internet and a modern web browser
- Your location details for dealer matching
- Contact information for inquiries

All steps take under 5 minutes total.

</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Setup

Follow these sequential steps to start using Sea-it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Your Local Dealer" icon="map-pin">
    Visit the dealer locator on the Sea-it platform.

    Use the search form with your city or postal code.

    ```bash
    # Example dealer search URL
    https://sea-it.com/dealers?location=your-city
    ```

    Review the list of certified dealers with contact details and distances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Stock Vehicles" icon="truck">
    Navigate to the stock vehicles section.

    Filter by trailer type, size, and availability.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Web Browser" icon="globe">
        Access full filters and high-resolution images.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
        Scan QR codes on listings for quick dealer contact.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit an Inquiry" icon="send">
    Select a vehicle and fill out the inquiry form.

    Provide your details and preferred delivery date.

    <Callout kind="tip">
      Include specific requirements like hitch type or load capacity for faster responses.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare for Delivery" icon="package">
    Receive confirmation email with tracking.

    Coordinate with your dealer on logistics.

    Expect delivery within 7-14 days based on stock status.
  </Step>
</Steps>
